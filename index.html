Get the app

Put Sudoku on your home screen. It opens full screen and works with no internet. It also checks for updates when you are online.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen on iPhone and at the top on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The Sudoku icon now sits on your home screen.

Android and computer

Tap Download app above and confirm. If it does nothing, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
